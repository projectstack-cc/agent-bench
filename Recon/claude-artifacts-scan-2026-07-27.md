## ROUND ONE — Chief Opportunity Officer: Research

**Approach:** ~16 distinct search queries against `claude.ai/public/
artifacts`, run in two shapes:
1. **Title/category search** — direct `site:` queries for common pain-
   point-adjacent categories (trackers, calculators, generators, business/
   client/workflow tools, CSV/PDF/OCR conversion, freelancer/CRM/invoice
   tooling, exam/certification prep, cold-outreach/personalization).
2. **Link-share search** — a second, independent shape hunting for people
   *linking* their artifact somewhere and explaining *why* they built it
   (Reddit's r/ClaudeAI, X/Twitter, Hacker News, generic "I made this
   because..." phrasing), on the theory that the real pain-point signal
   would be in the surrounding narrative, not the artifact title alone.

**Finding: the source does not yield what the directive was looking for,
and this is a property of the source, not a coverage gap.**

`claude.ai/public/artifacts` is a **revealed-preference surface with the
narrative stripped out.** People publish the artifact — a title, sometimes
a one-line description — not the complaint that led to it. Sixteen
queries across both search shapes surfaced the same small, repeating set
of ~8 unique artifacts, all bare titles with no attached "why I built
this" context:

- Research Funding Opportunity Tracker
- Solar PV LCOE Calculator
- CSV Upload And Viewer
- Daily Target Tracker — Bank PO Exam Study Planner
- Daily Budget Calculator
- Healthy Meal Planner & Recipe App
- AI Market Research Outreach Assistant (by a named consultant, Sanjeev
  Chaodhari — CSV-upload batch cold-outreach personalization tool)

The link-share round (Reddit, X, Hacker News, generic phrase search) came
back **equally thin** — general Anthropic press coverage and product
documentation, not people sharing their own artifact with a stated
grievance behind it. Two independent search shapes converging on the same
non-result is the signal this is genuinely how thin the source is, not a
query-craft problem worth cycling further on (Core Principle 3: every
recommendation needs measurable pain, a citation — not an inferred guess
at what a bare title implies about the builder's frustration).

**Per Core Principle 1 (never invent demand) and the Global Constitution's
Rule 1 (never invent user sentiment):** I cannot honestly assert "person X
built this because Y frustrated them" from a title alone. Doing so would
be exactly the fabricated-pain-point failure mode this company's own
standing lessons exist to prevent.

### The one candidate with an actual stated workflow behind it

**AI Market Research Outreach Assistant** — the single hit with more than
a bare title: a named AI market-research consultant built a CSV-upload,
batch-personalization cold-outreach tool for themselves. This is a real,
if thin (one data point, one builder), signal that manual cold-outreach
personalization is annoying enough for a solo consultant to self-build a
fix rather than buy one.

**Checked against Automatic Rejections and scored:** cold-outreach/lead-
gen tooling is a heavily funded, crowded category (Instantly, Smartlead,
Clay, Apollo, Lemlist, and dozens more, none independently re-verified
this session but well-known to be a saturated space) — the same crowding
shape this company's last four market scans converged on independently
(see `patterns.md`). One artifact from one builder is not a demand
signal under Core Principle 3; it is one person's revealed preference,
not evidence a market of similar buyers exists and is underserved.
**Not advanced to Round 2.** Named here rather than silently dropped, per
this company's own standard for an honestly-reported null.

### Two second-order observations, worth filing even though nothing advances

1. **This source is structurally weak for pain-point discovery, distinct
   from why the last four scans failed.** Those scans (PC gaming,
   e-commerce, 13 business functions, 3 function×vertical intersections)
   found *real, citable pain, already vendor-served* — a crowding
   failure. This scan found **artifacts with no attributable pain at
   all** — an evidence failure, one step earlier in the funnel. Different
   failure mode, worth distinguishing in `patterns.md` so a future session
   doesn't re-try this exact source expecting a different result.
2. **The artifacts that did surface sit in maximally saturated consumer
   categories** — a budget calculator, a meal planner, a study planner.
   Thousands of existing apps already serve each. This isn't evidence of
   an open gap; it's evidence that for a five-minute personal need,
   *building beats searching* now that Claude/artifacts make building
   free and instant. That's a real, if unrelated-to-this-founder,
   observation about AI-assisted one-off tooling eroding the long tail of
   consumer app markets — filed as a pattern, not a product lead.

**COO recommendation for Round 2: none. No candidate clears the evidence
bar. Recommend not re-running this exact source again without a
materially different search strategy (e.g., direct authenticated browsing
of the gallery rather than blind web search — this session's tooling
could not reach `madewithclaude.com`/`claudeartifacts.net`, both of which
403'd or failed to load, and may hold richer signal than what indexed
search surfaced).**

## FINAL VERDICT

**No candidate cleared the bar. This session's real output is a
characterization of the source, not a rejected product list.**

`claude.ai/public/artifacts`, searched via blind web indexing, surfaces
artifact titles without the surrounding narrative that would make them
usable as pain-point evidence. It is not a reliable opportunity-discovery
channel for this company as currently accessible — distinct from (and a
different failure mode than) the four prior scans, which found real pain
already served by funded competitors. If this source is worth
revisiting, it should be through direct, authenticated access to the
gallery/browse surface (not blind search), which this session's tooling
could not reach.

**Standing recommendation reaffirmed:** per the 2026-07-20 methodology
finding, domains where the founder has real, non-searchable operational
information asymmetry continue to outperform blind external scans —
including, now, this one.
