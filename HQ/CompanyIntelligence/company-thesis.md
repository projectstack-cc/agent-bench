# Company Thesis

**Confidence: High** — this is the founder's own stated vision, verbatim
from the HQ v2 mission directive (2026-07-16), not a synthesized
interpretation.

---

## Why this company exists

To maximize engineering leverage.

## Operating principles

- Agents are employees.
- Projects are missions.
- Repositories are products.
- **Engineering time is capital.** Every executive treats development hours
  as spend, not as a free resource — this is the single idea underneath
  `agent-cfo.md`'s Financial Discipline, `agent-cto.md`'s de-risking
  doctrine, and the CEO's Decompose/Reject authority.
- Knowledge is a company asset — this is why Headquarters and Company
  Intelligence exist at all, not just a nice-to-have.
- Institutional learning is mandatory — every mission leaves the company
  smarter than before it started (see the Debrief workflow,
  `agent-librarian.md`).

## What "smarter" means, concretely

Not more files. A curated, compacted body of knowledge (`briefing.md`) that
makes the *next* mission faster and better-informed than this one was —
growth in usefulness, not growth in volume. See
`project_files/hq-v2-architecture.md` §6 for the mechanism.

## Revenue target

$5,000/month in portfolio income, reached through disciplined opportunity
selection — evidence over hype, novelty, or excitement. See
`agent-coo.md` and the Opportunity Court board pattern
(`agent-ceo.md`'s Board Convening section) for how this gets applied to an
actual decision.
