## New methodology: technology-enabled whitespace discovery (started 2026-08-04)

**Confidence: Medium-High — direct founder direction.** After six
consecutive demand-side research passes all converged on "real pain,
already vendor-served" (the standing finding in `patterns.md`), the
founder directed a fundamentally different approach: instead of
searching for pain someone already identified, start from capability —
what technologies crossed the productizability threshold in the last
12–18 months, and what product categories are now technically possible
that weren't before?

**Opportunity Court IV ran same day** — the first court to use this
methodology. Five candidates entered; two survived the full board
gauntlet. Full report: `Recon/opportunity-court-4-report-2026-08-04.md`.

**Verdict: GO on Voice-First Field Intelligence (VFFI) as a research
candidate, not a build commitment.** Field workers (electricians,
plumbers, HVAC techs) dictate what happened on a job site; the AI
structures it into project updates, time logs, material lists, and
client reports — without anyone touching a keyboard. Enabled by Whisper
(free, accurate speech-to-text) + structured output (reliable JSON from
LLMs), both of which crossed the productizability threshold in 2024.
**No voice-first + AI-structured data capture tool exists for trades** —
Procore, BuilderTrend, and Fieldwire are all keyboard/tablet-first and
enterprise/mid-market; voice dictation apps (Dragon, Otter) don't
structure output into business data. The intersection is empty.

**Why it won on every dimension:** clean stack fit (React + Cloudflare +
Whisper + LLM), 91% gross margin at $39/mo with $3.50 variable cost,
daily-use engagement pattern (low churn), low liability (operational
data, not advisory), reachable buyer (trade associations, not
app-store-style distribution), and founder fit (field-operations
experience at XPanda Foam). Path to $5K/mo: 130 users (0.005% of the
2.5M US trade businesses).

**Strong second: Contract Transparency Tool (CTT)** — plain-language
contract risk analysis for non-lawyers. Genuine whitespace, clear buyer,
but liability risk and occasional-use pattern make it a harder first
product. Worth revisiting as a second product after VFFI establishes
revenue.

**Three rejected:** Knowledge Decay Monitor (latent problem, feature-of-
Notion risk), Dynamic Equipment Knowledge Base (multimodal AI not ready
for specialized schematics), Personal Decision Intelligence Platform
(niche practice, slow feedback loop, small market).

**Next step: a wedge-research spike, not a build commitment.** Three
concrete tests: (1) terminology accuracy test — feed 10 real electrician
field-work descriptions through the pipeline, kill switch if accuracy
below 80%; (2) buyer validation — talk to 5–10 trade business owners
about paperwork burden; (3) competitive check — verify no one has built
this since the last court. If all three pass, scope a Milestone 1 MVP
(electricians only, one beta user, 4–6 week build). If buyer validation
fails, fall back to CTT.

**The methodology itself is a finding.** Technology-enabled whitespace
discovery is a third methodology, distinct from pain-point mining and
demand-first search. When demand-pull methods converge on "already
served," technology-push methods can surface genuinely novel candidates
that demand-side methods cannot — because people don't complain about
not having something they don't know is possible. Filed in `patterns.md`.
